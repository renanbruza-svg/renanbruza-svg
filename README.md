### About Me

I am Renan Bruzarosco, a Bioprocess and Biotechnology Engineer transitioning into Data Science with a clear focus on healthcare, HealthTech, and applied artificial intelligence.

I am currently pursuing a postgraduate specialization in AI and Data Science applied to healthcare at Hospital Sirio-Libanes. My goal is to combine biological knowledge, quantitative thinking, and data-driven tools to help solve real problems in medical, operational, and health-related contexts.

My work is centered on Python, Pandas, SQL, exploratory data analysis, statistical reasoning, and machine learning foundations. I am especially interested in projects that connect healthcare data, predictive modeling, clinical indicators, public health datasets, and decision support.

### What I Am Building Toward

I am developing a portfolio that shows more than technical exercises. I want each project to communicate a real problem, the data used, the analytical reasoning, the model or method applied, and the practical recommendation that comes from the results.

My current direction is to build evidence of:

- Strong SQL and Python fundamentals
- Clear healthcare-related business questions
- Well-documented data cleaning and analysis
- Metrics that are easy to interpret
- Visualizations that support decisions
- Practical conclusions, not only model scores
